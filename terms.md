Ski Viral Engine ("the app") is a private tool operated by Florian, Sweden, used to edit and publish skiing videos to the operator's own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

1. Use of the app
The app is used only by its operator to manage their own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

2. Content and permissions
Videos are only published when the original creator has given permission. The original creator is credited in every post. Content is removed on request from the rights holder.

3. TikTok and Instagram
Publishing happens through TikTok's and Instagram's official APIs. Use of those platforms is also governed by their own terms. The account owner selects the privacy settings and approves each post before it is published.

4. Music
Only music that is licensed for this use, or added through the platforms' own music libraries, is used.
