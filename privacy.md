Ski Viral Engine ("the app") is a private tool operated by Florian, Sweden.

What data we access
When the account owner connects TikTok or Instagram, the app receives an access token and basic account information (such as user ID and username). It uses these only to publish videos the owner has approved and to check their upload status.

Where data is stored
All data, including access tokens, video files and publishing history, is stored locally on the operator's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Third-party services
The app connects to the official APIs of TikTok, Instagram (Meta), YouTube (Google), Last.fm and Jamendo. Those services handle data according to their own privacy policies.

Creators
For videos we feature, we store the creator's public username, the video link and the permission they gave us. Creators can ask us to delete this information and remove the post at any time.

Deleting your data
The account owner can disconnect TikTok or Instagram at any time in the platform's settings, which revokes the app's access. To request deletion of any data, contact us.
